Privacy Policy

PlayFlix Download Manager & Browser Extension | Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: PlayFlix Download Manager does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal data, browsing habits, or downloaded content. All downloading happens directly between your browser, the local app, and the target server.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

PlayFlix operates on a zero-telemetry philosophy:

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, location).
  • We do not record or monitor your browsing history.
  • We do not log the contents, URLs, or metadata of the files you download.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third-party analytics or advertising networks.

2. Browser Extension Permissions & Usage

The PlayFlix browser companion extension requests permissions strictly to provide download acceleration and media detection features:

  • Native Messaging: Allows the extension to communicate with the locally installed desktop application to offload download requests to high-speed native download threads.
  • Web Request & All URLs: Enables detecting downloadable media streams (such as video and audio segments) embedded on web pages you browse, enabling the "Download this video" button.
  • Cookies: Used solely to pass necessary session authentication cookies to the download engine so authenticated downloads do not fail with access errors. Cookies are processed entirely in-memory on your machine and never transmitted externally.
  • Downloads: Allows catching file downloads initiated from the browser and delegating them to PlayFlix.
  • Storage: Stores your local extension settings (such as file type filters and dark mode preferences) within your local browser storage.

3. Local Storage and Data Security

All download queues, file histories, and user settings are stored locally on your own computer storage. No external servers or cloud databases are involved in your file transactions.

4. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices evolve, updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
